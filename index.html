The formula earns trust against files you can download today (weights = params × bpw ÷ 8 × 1.08; the 8% is embeddings and the output head staying above the quant's nominal bits):

The math, stated

  • Weights = params × 10⁹ × (quant bpw ÷ 8) × 1.08. The bpw column is llama.cpp's nominal table; the 8% fudge is what real files show once embeddings and lm_head refuse to shrink.
  • KV cache = 2 × layers × kv_heads × head_dim × 2 bytes × context. Head dim is 128 across every preset above. This is why MHA Llama-2 eats 4 GiB of cache at 8k while GQA Llama-3 eats 1.
  • Overhead = CUDA context + cuBLAS + compute graph buffers. ~0.6–1.2 GiB; it grows a little with context and a lot with batch size.
  • What it doesn't count: MoE routing tables (often sharded oddly), draft models, and the VRAM your desktop already took.

We run CPU-side inference benches and publish the ledgers; the GPU math here is consolidation of public quant tables and public file sizes — no "we tested it on an H100" cosplay.
